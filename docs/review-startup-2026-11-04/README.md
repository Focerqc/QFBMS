## Cold attach and restart

Each local half-stack feeds BAT through its own diode/filter and the preregulator collector through a separate diode. BREG controls the NPN base, R19/R119 provide 10 MΩ discharge, REGIN has local 22 nF, REG1 has 1 µF. REG18 decouples locally. All upper returns end at CELL16. With a valid BAT, an awake BQ7695202 and factory defaults, REG1 comes up at nominal 3.3 V before the first I²C write. Both monitors use CRC. Initial attach can leave the part awaiting a documented wake event. Check the actual order code, OTP and POR/wake state on both parts. Require REGIN ≥4.1 V and REG1 within 3.0–3.6 V at 0–45 mA. Check 22 nF effective capacitance after tolerance, DC bias and aging against TI's >7 nF requirement. [TI §§5, 7.11–12 and 16.2](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq76952.pdf).

The draft starts the ESP from an independent, CELL0-referenced, regulated bench source at J201. Closing maintained SW201 connects AUX_3V3 to HOST_3V3 even with both BQs in SHUTDOWN. Neither BQ supplies the ESP. With the auxiliary source absent there is no autonomous restart. **OPEN — production host supply.** Select an independent source and start control for the real pack/charger range, then verify reverse current, USB paths, cold start, contact resistance, hold-up, input protection and hot standby drain. Do not infer an operating full-stack converter from the main PCB.

For SHUTDOWN recovery, first power the host, then press SW202. Both outputs operate from HOST_3V3 while REG1_BQ1 and REG1_BQ2 are zero. Release the contact, wait for BQ initialization, both local rails and the isolators' hot-swap/idle conditions, then enumerate. A closed or stuck contact can prevent SHUTDOWN re-entry. This manual circuit requires an operator. Automatic charger wake would need a separately qualified pulse source. DEEPSLEEP has its own exit command/conditions. [TI power modes and stacked wake](https://www.ti.com/lit/pdf/sszt191).

## Admission, firmware and interrupted startup

With R204 fitted, 10 kΩ R203 pulls U204.9 and GPIO9 high during high-impedance reset. Low drive sinks ≤0.364 mA through a 1% pull at 3.6 V. Verify VIH at both pins during CHIP_EN reset, its release and at least 3 ms strap hold. SPI boot samples GPIO9 high. Joint download requires GPIO9 low and GPIO8 high, with GPIO2 pull-up recommended. **Remove R204 before entering download**, then strap GPIO9 at the ESP. R203 independently holds OE high. A fitted link plus a forced low admits the upper bus regardless of firmware. Restore the link before normal startup. Verify programming fixtures and any ROM pin activity. [ESP32-C3 §3](https://www.espressif.com/sites/default/files/documentation/esp32-c3_datasheet_en.pdf) · [TMUX154E](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tmux154e.pdf).

Normal firmware drives GPIO9 high, initializes lower at 0x08, writes 0x20 as the address configuration, swaps mode, then reads lower Cell2Voltage using CRC at 7-bit 0x10. Only successful verification permits GPIO9 low and upper initialization at 0x08. Failed writes or three unsuccessful verification attempts leave the switch open. A retry starts with admission revoked and handles a lower already at 0x10. Repeated Lisp requests must pass the same C guard. Wake requires no new pin assignment. GPIO4/8 retain their legacy owners, GPIO2 remains the button input, and GPIO9 exclusively requests upper admission in run mode.

**OPEN — brownout while the ESP remains alive.** A resistor cannot overrule an actively driven low. A lower reset can restore 0x08 while the upper remains visible and the software still holds a stale verification flag. Require loss-of-power detection or an independent interlock that opens U204 before address ambiguity. In the maintained target, revoke verification on rail loss, timeout, failed CRC or monitor reset, stop charging, open the switch, wait for bus idle and qualified power, and rerun the complete serialized initialization with bounded retries. Confirm volatile sensing, balancing, address and charge configuration by readback. Check power-down admission before SHUTDOWN. Boot must tolerate a delayed manual pulse without latching success. Current source checks model address failures and retries only. Close with forced independent BQ resets, ESP brownouts and bus interruption at every phase, observing OE and both physical buses.

## Power and lower bus margins

U206 uses ISO1640BDR side 1 on HOST_3V3, side 2 on lower REG1, both grounds on CELL0. Each side has 100 nF. The dielectric barrier breaks the DC SDA/SCL path even with a common return. U204 gates only the upper branch. R205/R206 supply the upstream lines independently of R201/R202 behind U204. R20/R21 and R120/R121 load only their respective REG1 rails.

At 3.0–3.6 V each ISO side 2 can draw 6.7 mA static plus two 4.7 kΩ ±1% pull currents ≤1.55 mA total. Each half-stack thus carries ~8.25 mA incremental load, leaving 36.75 mA before switching and other loads. At 67.36 V per half and REGIN 5.5 V, the pass device dissipates about 0.51 W from this load alone, or 2.78 W at 45 mA. Internal drop dissipates ~18 mW at 8.25 mA. **OPEN** both pass devices/diodes, SOA, headroom, effective capacitance, junction temperature in the case and upper/lower drain mismatch. Verify component working ratings at actual stack/transient maxima, startup gain and hot copper thermal impedance. On HOST_3V3 an illustrative coincident load includes 335 mA ESP RF peak, 2 × 7.1 mA ISO side 1, ~3.24 mA external bus pulls, 0.36 mA OE bias and <16 mA wake drive. The resulting ~369 mA excludes dynamic margin and peripherals. Start with an independent 0.5 A or larger bench source and capture pin-level droop during TX, switch closure and wake. [ESP §5.6](https://www.espressif.com/sites/default/files/documentation/esp32-c3_datasheet_en.pdf) · [ISO1640 §§6.9–12](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/iso1640.pdf).

**Partial power.** With both off there is no live pull. With host only or BQ only, each live pull ends at its powered port. Table 8-2 specifies high impedance if VCC1 <2.3 V or VCC2 <1.7 V. It leaves intermediate ramp behavior unresolved. For valid supplies, ISO input leakage ≤10 µA per pin at local VCC gives ≤44 mV drop on a 4.3 kΩ pull or ≤48 mV on 4.7 kΩ. That limit does not bound an externally driven pin above a dead or ramping rail. Even a hypothetical short to a local clamp can draw up to 0.846 mA per upstream pull or 0.774 mA per local/downstream pull at 3.6 V. A push-pull driver is not resistor limited. With a common local supply those same-side pulls decay with the victim rail. Examine residual charge, external programmers, internal ESP pulls, reset driver mode and unequal pin/rail decay. U204 powered-off IOFF ±2 µA applies only under its specified zero-supply test conditions. No evidence guarantees zero injection through intermediate supplies. **OPEN** until the supported pin/rail envelope and allowable injected current are documented for every external drive state. Sweep both orders up/down and pause at intermediate voltages, with drivers low, released and in reset. Measure supply and I/O currents, any dead-rail revival, and absolute and recommended limits. Keep independent supplies current limited; qualify the production topology if USB can power any pin separately. [ISO1640 §§6.3, 6.9, 8.6](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/iso1640.pdf).

**Loaded logic.** At host minimum 3.0 V, R205/R206 max 4343 Ω give (3.0−0.710)/4343 = 0.527 mA with U204 open, within ISO side-1 specified 0.5–3.5 mA window before leakage. With U204 closed both pulls feed one sink. At maximum rail and even zero VOL their sum is ≤1.62 mA/line, plus ~80 µA typical ESP internal pull. Internal 45 kΩ is typical only. Disable internal pulls in the eventual firmware or guarantee their parallel load, and verify leakage leaves the 27 µA low-current margin. With U204 open the downstream 4.7 kΩ pair alone can supply only 0.482 mA at the 0.710 V corner. Do not rely on upper-side-1 VOL before admission. Across U204 a conservative 10 Ω test-point estimate gives ≤8 mV toward the upper receiver or ≤10 mV on an upper return to the host; only the far-side pull current crosses the switch.

ESP-driven zeros must be ≤0.480 V at U206 side 1 and approximately ≤0.472 V before U204 for U_ISO. ISO side-2 VOL ≤0.4 V at ≥0.5 mA; (3.0−0.4)/4747=0.548 mA. BQ low threshold 0.33 × REG18 gives ≥0.528 V at REG18=1.6 V. In reverse, the local BQ must sink below 0.3 × VCC2, ≥0.900 V at 3.0 V. BQ's 0.77 V specification at 5 V/5 mA does not guarantee VOL at 3.3 V. Host-bound ISO VOL is 0.570–0.710 V in the specified current window. ESP VIL at the 3.3 V/25 °C test point gives 0.825 V, about 115 mV lower or 105 mV upper return margin. ESP loaded output and input thresholds over temperature and supply remain **OPEN**. Verify idle high exceeds 0.75 VDD at the ESP test point, 0.7 VCC1 on side 1, 0.5 VCC2 on side 2 and 0.66 REG18 at each BQ. [November 4 electrical review and test conditions](../review-2026-11-04/electrical-review.md).

**Which receiver matters.** With U204 open the ESP drives address, write bits, START/repeated START, master ACK during reads and clocks into U206 side 1. U_BQ1 receives these from side 2. Slave ACK/read zeros and stretched SCL go from U_BQ1 into U206 side 2, then to the ESP. With U204 closed master lows must reach both side-1 inputs and both BQs. The addressed BQ alone returns ACK/read data or stretches; its local ISO input and then the ESP must recognize that low. The other ISO side 1 need not relay the slave's ACK/data to an inactive BQ. Its guaranteed low ceiling is 0.480 V, below the offset low by 90–230 mV. An inactive peer may see stretch release early. Exercise transitions between slaves, final NACK, STOP, repeated START, CRC and stretches with the ESP actually waiting for SCL high. Bus transparency between peers is not guaranteed.

**100 kHz timing.** For a 60 pF upstream budget (include both ISO inputs, switch, ESP, traces and probe), 0.8473 RC gives 221 ns at 4343 Ω with U204 open, ~115 ns when closed at equivalent 2268 Ω. Require ≤80 pF on side 1. On a local bus at 80 pF and 4747 Ω expect 322 ns 30–70% but 834 ns 10–90%. TI's 1 µs 10–90% SCL rise limit constrains local capacitance to ~96 pF despite ISO's 400 pF rating. If the BQ is in 400 kHz mode, 300 ns limits it to ~29 pF. Measure at the receiving pins, with SCL fall ≤300 ns. For standard mode confirm low ≥4.7 µs, high ≥4.0 µs, data setup ≥250 ns, repeated START setup ≥4.7 µs, STOP setup ≥4.0 µs and bus free ≥4.7 µs. A nominal 10 µs period leaves 1.3 µs beyond minimum high/low. Include both propagation directions and stretch release, with actual RC and probe load. OPEN pending scoped margins at supply and temperature extremes. [TI BQ §§7.27–28](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq76952.pdf).

## Wake assertion, release and qualification

SW202 alone owns WAKE_PULSE. When open, R208 drains Q201 base through 47 kΩ to CELL0. The upper LED has no source. With both REG1 rails off and HOST_3V3 valid, pressing SW202 drives Q201 via R207 and the LED via R209. Q201.3 pulls lower TS2 to Q201.2/CELL0. U207.4 pulls upper TS2 to U207.3/CELL16. Host reset cannot assert either path. A held button continues asserting through ESP reset; loss of HOST_3V3 removes drive.

Assuming 1% resistors, 3.0 V rail, ≤0.2 V contact drop and illustrative VBE 0.8 V, net base current ≥(2.8−0.8)/3333−0.8/46530 ≈0.58 mA. Maximum series current <1.11 mA. Vendor saturation at 10 mA collector/0.5 mA base is a 25 °C check only. Upper LED with 1.5 V maximum VF at the 5 mA test point has ≥5.17 mA available; even a short draws <14.61 mA. CTR bin 100–200% and VCEsat ≤0.4 V at IF=5 mA, IC=1.25 mA give a room-temperature assertion check. U207's barrier VIORM 707 Vpeak compares with nominal maximum 16 × 4.21 = 67.36 V between grounds. Its transistor VCEO 80 V compares separately with at most ~6 V from local TS2 to CELL16. **OPEN** transients, creepage, package/footprint, pollution and insulation requirements. [Vishay pp. 1–5](https://www.vishay.com/docs/84433/voma617a.pdf) · [onsemi BC847 series](https://www.onsemi.com/pdf/datasheet/bc847att1-d.pdf).

With LD below its wake threshold and BAT valid, command SHUTDOWN, verify REG1=0 on both halves, apply a 2 ms trial pulse to SW202 and measure each pin 22 relative to its own VSS. Require <0.7 V for ≥200 µs, release >1.1 V, both devices awake, successful enumeration and subsequent re-entry to SHUTDOWN. Repeat with minimum BAT, both rail corners, contact bounce, host initially off, reset while pressed and interrupted wake, at −40/25/85 °C. Test dark output at local 6 V and 85 °C with a guarded instrument. Require **≤0.10 µA total released leakage per assembled TS2 path**, including PCB and measurement burden. The typical 4.6 MΩ internal source provides only ~0.41 µA at 3 V down to 1.1 V. Room-temperature opto dark current or typical hot curves cannot qualify this. **OPEN** until a guaranteed hot limit or qualified production screen plus hot-soak false-wake and shutdown-entry results exist for both paths. [TI §§7.3, 7.6 and 13.5](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq76952.pdf).
