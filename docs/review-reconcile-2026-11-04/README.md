# QFBMS board reconciliation | 4 November 2026

**Do not fabricate or energize this revision.** The only retired PCB component is Q2. Neither its gate nor its source has another *assigned* PCB endpoint. Its drain is shared with the charge circuit; two copper graphics physically intersect the footprint and other retained pads. Removing Q2 eliminates the unused output branch, not the existing shorts or the need for a working charge disconnect.

Started clean on `feature/bq-monitors` at `4575007f85af5ec796bccab51e511531217a5d9e`. [Start/worktrees](start.json) records the initial clean status separately from the later untracked evidence directory. Board change: `8fcb7d6`. Separate monitor schematic change: `aaf2c3b`. No netlist import, historical generator, zone refill, main schematic edit, change to the supplied VBMS32 sources, or change to the maintained adaptation.

## What survived the review

[Component ledger (CSV)](component-ledger.csv) has one decision for each of the 150 starting references. Each row includes its actual physical pad assignments (including repeated numbers and blank pads), function, disposition, specific next check, touching tracks/graphics and baseline DRC counts on its pad/footprint IDs. Shape or field reports on child graphics can occur in addition to those counts. **83 required** means the site or function is needed, not that the present part or circuit is qualified. **65 unresolved** remain in place. **One replacement needed** refers to the R_SSR2 connection, not a selected substitute. **One confirmed obsolete** was removed.

The [before](board-before.json) and [after](board-after.json) inventories distinguish assigned nets from topology. [Direct copper contacts](pad-copper-before.json) intersect real pad and track/via/board-graphic shapes on a common enabled copper layer, including foreign and blank nets. These are direct intersections, not bench continuity or a complete path through other objects. Look up a copper UUID in the objects map, then the pads touching it. [After contacts](pad-copper-after.json) retain identical contacts on every surviving pad. The [native fixed-footprint snapshot](protected-native-before.json) includes drills, shapes, layers, mask, models, position, rotation and side; all 45 full blocks still match. Both BQs are unmoved. The [comparison](board-comparison.txt) covers all 149 surviving footprints; **zero surviving pad assignments changed**, 655 physical pads became 646. The nine removed pads were Q2.1 (gate), .2–.8 (PACK+) and .D (fused top).

- **Switching and protection.** J_BAT1 is on CELL32/CELL0; J_CHG1 is on CHG+/CHG-. Assigned positive path: J_CHG1.1 → Q1 sources → Q1 drain → F1.2 → F1.1 → CELL32. Q2 had the same fused drain but led to PACK+, not CHG+; the read-only main schematic explicitly drives Q2 through R_G2 from SSR_OUT_DSG. The supplied GPIO4/8 output and precharge APIs do not require a second power output here. R_DSG1 has neither an assigned net nor a touching copper object. No evidence equates it to R_G2; it stays. Q1, D_GS1, R_GS1, both parallel gate-series candidates and the shared U_SSR1 bypass network remain. GSRELAY on an H2440-named footprint is not a verified MPN or pinout.
- **Why Q1 is still open.** The [AGM drawing, rev. 2.71 p. 1](../../_reference/pinouts/agm_ds.pdf) shows gate 1, sources 2–8, drain 9 (PCB calls it D), 150 V VDS and ±20 V VGS. With the indicated N-channel body diode the charger can feed the pack through source-to-drain even when the gate is off. The existing circuit also physically shorts parts of that intended path. Select and verify an off-state topology, reverse-polarity behavior, GPIO5/10 truth table, reset/off bias, gate clamp and bounded loss-of-host shutdown. R_SSR2 pulls Q1_GATE toward GND rather than the moving CHG+ source; replace this **connection** in that design. Do not treat nominal 134 V/5 A as a transient limit. Establish DC fuse breaking capacity, hot SOA, clamp energy and enclosed temperature without a heatsink.
- **Cell sensing and balancing.** The 32 assigned R_F inputs run from CELL32 down to CELL1; C_F1/C_F17 return to CELL0/CELL16 respectively, and each remaining capacitor spans adjacent filtered inputs. The live file has all 32 C_F on **F.Cu, 0402**, contrary to the back-side description in earlier documentation. No capacitor was moved. Qualify effective capacitance and the preferred 0603 land pattern against available space. All 33 R_B sites are fixed, front-side, 27R0 marked and both pads unassigned. Keep every site but do not infer 33 channels: map the actual 32 channels, select internal/external balancing, estimate bleed current/temperature and test the host mask including the stack boundary. Harness contacts are J_CELL1 pins 1–10 CELL9→0, J_CELL2 pins 1–9 CELL18→10, J_CELL3 pins 1–8 CELL26→19, J_CELL4 pins 1–6 CELL32→27. Photos IMG_2143–2151 and the harness drawing support connector function and case constraints, not mating-side numbering or continuity. Do not use the printed ranges as the wiring map.
- **Both monitor domains.** U_BQ1.16 is CELL0, U_BQ2.16 is CELL16, but both .17 are GND, both .18/.20 share SRP/SRN and both .49 EP are unset. Localize upper VSS, EP, unused current inputs, decoupling, BAT, REGIN/BREG, REG18/REG1, wake and communications to CELL16. Only the lower monitor measures charge current. Check each local half-stack against [TI's pin table and application circuit](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq76952.pdf) and [stacked-monitor guidance](https://www.ti.com/lit/pdf/sszt191). The draft's separate BAT and preregulator feeds and two local rail domains do not identify D_PRI/D_SEC on this board. Those six diode sites need reference-board tracing, polarity, MPN, headroom/leakage, pulse ratings and thermal qualification.
- **Current and voltage.** The assigned negative path is CELL0 → R_SH1 → SHUNT_MID → R_SH2 → CHG-. Each is marked R001; neither SRP nor SRN is assigned to either resistor. The physical graphics also overlap terminals. PCB_VERSION 2 returns lower CC2/100 without a sign inversion in C; Lisp multiplies by −0.2 and treats negative application current as charging. With the C gain for 0.2 mΩ, ideal application magnitude scales as 0.2 × effective sensed resistance / 0.2 mΩ: a 1 mΩ pickup would cancel the multiplier, while 2 mΩ would double the reading. This is not a choice of shunt. Establish true Kelvin points, sign, DC resistance and calibration by injection. Charger/output ADC channels assume 220k/4.7k (ratio 47.81; ~2.80 V at 134 V). No implemented divider to U_ESP1 is established. Size series working-voltage ratings, ADC attenuation, protection and references; verify both channels against independent meters.
- **Temperatures.** J_T1–4 return to GND and are named TS1–4. J_T2 on the lower BQ's pin 22 conflicts with TS2 wake; J_T4 on the upper BQ does not implement the package's fourth lower sensor. The code configures lower TS1, TS3, ALERT and DCHG for up to four cell NTCs and HDQ for the MOS NTC; retain the connectors. Verify 10 kΩ beta3380, 18 kΩ internal pull-up and 500 Ω series correction against the real assembly. Test open/short and absent-sensor handling; the 24 °C fallback is not protection.
- **Host and supply.** U4, L1, D_BST1, C_TAN1 and the other unassigned storage/filter/steering parts remain OPEN. An LTC7138 value and nearby inductor are not an implemented supply. [Analog Devices specifies a 140 V, 400 mA buck](https://www.analog.com/en/products/ltc7138.html); 134 V nominal leaves little input headroom and the draft's illustrative ~369 mA host load leaves little output margin. Establish the actual input domain, operating/transient range, UVLO, saturation current, bootstrap, thermal dissipation, reverse feed, start energy and standby imbalance. Never connect a 100 V C_FLT1 across the full pack. Bench J201/SW201 start the draft host independently of REG1; they do not select a production supply.
- **ESP, CAN, USB and programming.** U_ESP1 has no assigned pads. From the [ESP32-C3-MINI-1 module pin table](https://www.espressif.com/sites/default/files/documentation/esp32-c3-mini-1_datasheet_en.pdf), SDA/SCL GPIO21/20 are pads 31/30, CAN TX/RX GPIO7/6 are 21/20, CHG/PSW GPIO5/10 are 19/16, button GPIO2 is 5, buzzer GPIO3 is 6, legacy GPIO4/8 are 18/22, and COM GPIO9 is 23. Charger/output GPIO0/1 are pads 12/13; USB D−/D+ GPIO18/19 are 26/27. This is a software-to-module cross-check, not new PCB assignments. Keep antenna clearance. Provide EN/reset, independent strap bias and programming access; remove the draft run link before forcing GPIO9 low. J_USB1 has no assigned signal pads. Verify both CC resistors, connector pin pairs, VBUS current/backfeed, ESD and shield return. U7 pins 1–4 are +3V3/RX/TX/GND, 5–8 ISO_GND/CANL/CANH/5VExt. [TI's ISO1050DUB](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/iso1050.pdf) needs a valid second supply; check land pattern, separate returns, termination, creepage, harness and transients. USB/CAN must not bypass charge sensing or off-state isolation.
- **Wake, address admission and fail-off.** The maintained C starts with GPIO9 high, configures lower 0x08 → 0x10, verifies Cell2Voltage with CRC before allowing low and upper 0x08, and guards repeated Lisp requests. The separate draft uses U206 for lower partial-power isolation and U204/U_ISO for the upper branch. SW202 is a manual pulse to two TS2 paths, each returning to its own VSS; no GPIO newly assigned. Qualify power order, REG1 availability, bus VOL/rise/stretch, partial-power injection and hot TS2 leakage/release. A lower reset while the ESP still drives OE low can invalidate prior address verification. Software admission tests do not prove independent brownout recovery, automatic wake, post-wake configuration readback or bounded charge-off on stale data. C disables automatic protection groups A/B; the Micro Lisp voltage, current, temperature and balance policy must run. Confirm runtime settings for the 32S1P Samsung 50S and 134 V/5 A charger.
