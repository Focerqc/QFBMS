# Electrical decisions · 4 November 2026

## Lower communication

**Disposition: open the two lower bus wires.** No lower switch or isolator is fitted. R20/R21 are value OPEN and terminate on REG1_BQ1. This makes powered-off isolation independent of rail order, at the cost of all lower communication. With the maintained firmware, lower address verification cannot pass. U204 stays open. No PCB import is warranted.

### Transfer ownership

The evaluated ISO1640BDR has pins 1/4 on HOST_3V3/CELL0, 8/5 on REG1_BQ1/CELL0, 2/3 toward the host, 7/6 toward lower SDA/SCL. Its upstream side would require its own host pull pair. R201/R202 are *behind* U204. They are unavailable upstream when U204 is open. The lower local pair R20/R21 and upper pair R120/R121 each load only their own REG1. The following bounds assume a proposed external pair on each of these four segments, never two pulls on the same isolated local line.

- U204 open: master drives START, lower default 0x08/W (wire byte 0x10), register and write data; lower drives each ninth-clock ACK. After the firmware writes 0x20 and applies the address change, verification uses write 0x10/W (0x20), command, repeated START, 0x10/R (0x21), lower ACK/data. Master drives intermediate read ACKs, releases SDA for the final NACK, then makes STOP (SDA rises with SCL high). No upper receiver participates. Failure to decode the lower ACK gives an observed NACK/verification failure, and the guard denies upper admission.
- U204 closed: both branches must receive master-driven START, address, command and data zeros, SCL lows, repeated START, master ACKs and STOP. The addressed slave alone drives its write ACKs or read ACK/data. For lower writes/reads the wire bytes are 0x20/0x21. For upper they are 0x10/0x11. A lower return low reaches the ESP directly via the lower ISO side 1; an upper return low reaches it via U204. In each case the **ESP** is the required receiver. The unaddressed BQ is not a consumer of the other slave's ACK or read data. A side-1 receiver missing an offset low is real but does not, by itself, demonstrate a failed read or write in this single-master sequence.
- A BQ can stretch SCL. The addressed device holds its local SCL low after the master releases it. Its ISO returns offset SCL to the ESP. If the ESP samples high early it can advance SDA, clock a wrong bit or issue repeated START/STOP too soon; observable results include CRC mismatch, NACK or bus timeout. The inactive peer can see SCL high early when it misses the offset. No deterministic failure from that fact alone was identified. Test first accesses after idle and transitions between addressed slaves. Fetching transfer-buffer subcommand results has a separate wait requirement. ### Electrical margins per wire

Trial rail envelope 3.0–3.6 V; actual host minimum including ripple is unverified. Assume 4.7 kΩ ±1%, Rmin=4653 Ω, Rmax=4747 Ω. The ESP pull is enabled; 45 kΩ is typical, with no min/max specified. The board is unrouted. Values below are conditional bounds, not measured pin voltages.

- **Side-1 current:** TI specifies 0.570–0.710 V only at 0.5–3.5 mA. U204 open, (3.0−0.710)/4747 = 0.482 mA on the single upstream pull. A typical internal pull adds 0.051 mA but cannot guarantee the floor. Require Rmax ≤4580 Ω or host min ≥3.084 V with 4747 Ω. A 4.3 kΩ ±1% upstream pull would give ≥0.527 mA before leakage. U204 closed, two external pulls supply about 0.965 mA at this corner (slightly less across RON). At zero VOL the external ceiling is 1.548 mA + typical internal 0.080 mA; at 0.570 V and 3.6 V it is 1.303 mA + typical 0.067 mA. To guarantee ≤3.5 mA even at zero, internal Rp must exceed 1.85 kΩ before leakage. Check both ends; 0.710 V must not be extrapolated below 0.5 mA.
- **U204 current:** use 10 Ω as an estimate bounded at TI's test point (VCC=3 V, VI=0.4 V, −8 mA). When the lower ISO or ESP sinks, only downstream R201/R202 current crosses U204, ≤0.774 mA or 7.74 mV. When the upper ISO sinks, only upstream external plus internal current crosses: ≤0.854 mA or ≤8.54 mV with typical internal Rp. At 0.710 V and 3.6 V the estimate is 0.685 mA, 6.85 mV. Its own local R201/R202 supplies the upper sink directly. Neither BQ pull flows through U204.
- **Host-driven zeros:** lower ISO SDA1/SCL1 require ESP loaded VOL ≤0.480 V. Upper ISO requires ≤0.472 V at the ESP with that series allowance. Espressif gives 0.33 V at high impedance at **3.3 V, 25 °C**; this suggests 150/142 mV only under that test condition. The 28 mA at VOL=0.495 V, PAD_DRIVER=3 is typical; 0.495 V already exceeds the ISO threshold. Verify the actual open-drain driver at its aggregate load. A push-pull high or active driver during reset must be included in partial-power testing.
- **Slave-driven zeros:** ESP VIL=0.825 V only at 3.3 V, 25 °C. Lower return margin 115 mV, upper about 106 mV including the conservative drop. No full-temperature or low-rail guarantee follows. Conversely a peer ISO side 1 has at best a guaranteed low ceiling of 0.480 V: 90–230 mV negative margin before series drop. The two branches cannot transparently forward each other's lows.
- **BQ side:** with the 4.7 kΩ local pull and 3.0 V, side-2 VOL=0.4 V draws ≥0.548 mA, within 0.5–50 mA. BQ VIL=0.33×REG18 gives a worst-case ceiling of 0.528 V at 1.6 V REG18, or ≥128 mV margin. BQ-to-ISO must sink below 0.3×3.0=0.900 V. TI's 0.77 V limit at REG1 configured to 5 V, IOL=5 mA, VBAT>8 V and 10 pF would give 130 mV at that condition; it does not establish actual 3.3 V BQ VOL. Check ACK and stretch. Local idle high ≥3.0 V vs BQ VIH≤1.32 V and ISO VIH2=0.5 VCC2. Host idle must exceed ISO recommended VIH1=0.7 VCC1 and ESP VIH=0.75 VDD at 3.3 V/25 °C.
- **Edges:** 30–70% tr=0.8473 Rp C. At 80 pF and 4747 Ω, 322 ns open or 161 ns with two parallel pulls. Respect side-1 limit 80 pF on each connected port (60 pF provisional budget). Count both ISO inputs, ESP, switch (7.5 pF typical on, 2 pF off), pads, protection, routing and probes. Side-2 limit 400 pF alone is insufficient: at 400 pF, one 4747 Ω pull gives 1.61 µs. TI BQ §7.27 measures **10–90%** rise: 2.197 Rp C gives 834 ns at 80 pF and a local single-pull ceiling of ~96 pF for 1 µs. Check SCL fall ≤300 ns. Confirm configured BQ mode; §7.28's 300 ns rise criterion gives ~29 pF if the faster mode applies.
- **REG1:** ISO side-2 static 6.7 mA max for the 3.3 V supply group plus two low pulls ≤1.55 mA gives 8.25 mA. This leaves 36.75 mA of a nominal 45 mA before dynamic and other loads. At 16×4.21 V, external pass dissipation ≈(67.36−5.5)×8.25 mA=0.51 W on that increment, or ~2.78 W at 45 mA. Internal LDO increment ≈18 mW. Balancing affects thermal/rail behavior separately. No qualified pass transistor, copper thermal impedance or minimum REGIN headroom yet. Measure BAT, REGIN, REG1, current and case temperature at high stack/hot ambient and minimum headroom. Require rail within guaranteed limits and junction below rated limit with margin.

### Power-state decision and bench closure

- Host only, BQ only, both off: ISO Table 8-2 specifies high-impedance I²C outputs when VCC1<2.3 V or VCC2<1.7 V. The barrier separates local pulls. High impedance does not specify zero leakage. Both supplies valid permit communication only after hot-swap startup and within operating limits. Shutdown, startup and brownout can traverse unspecified regions; the lockout row cannot be extended through those regions.
- **Bootstrap failure:** with default-off REG1 the lower ISO cannot pass the first 0x08 write enabling REG0/REG1. The maintained bq_init writes REG0Config and REG12Config over that same bus. The lower never gets to 0x10 verification. The retained upper path has the same dependence. Establish actual suffix/OTP enabling both supplies *before* the first transaction, or provide a separately qualified local interface supply and prove injection safety with the monitor off.
- A single TMUX154EDGSR on lower REG1, host at A/B, has ±2 µA IOFF specified with VCC=0, An/Bn=0, A/B=0–4.3 V and controls at zero. With REG1 valid, host off, the enabled pass path can energize host pins. Powering it from host swaps the failing case. IOZ ±1 µA is specified at VCC=4.3 V, switch off. A separate dual-rail enable/UVLO, rail discharge and injection budget would be needed. With both valid a passive path also passes upper offset lows toward the inactive lower BQ. Direct copper provides no isolation. No lower switch selected.
- At bench, ramp each supply up/down first, interrupt mid-transfer, hold at intermediate voltages and toggle host drivers. Probe rails/current, ESP GPIO20/21, both sides of U204 pins 6/8 and 4/2, and BQ pins 26/27 relative to each local VSS. Require no unintended rail revival and compliance with vendor pin/injection limits for the selected power architecture. Require both current bounds, receiver thresholds and edge criteria above, good CRC/ACK, host wait through stretch, and fresh verified 0x10 before every upper admission after restart. Include both rail corners and temperature. A sample pass cannot establish a missing manufacturer guarantee. ALERT/LD/RST_SHUT and non-I²C host supplies need separate integration review.

## TS2 with REG1 inactive

BAT powers a weak local source in SHUTDOWN. At BAT>6 V the open-circuit level is 4.5–6 V, at BAT 4.7–6 V it can be 3–6 V. The 4.6 MΩ source impedance is typical. (5−1.1)/4.6 MΩ ≈0.85 µA is an estimate, not a leakage guarantee; at 3 V the same estimate gives only 0.41 µA. TS2 must release to enter SHUTDOWN. Both raw sheet ports stay open; neither is a logic-level GPIO.

- **Lower evaluation:** BC847BTT1G collector to U_BQ1.22, emitter to CELL0, trial 3.3 kΩ ±1% base series and 47 kΩ ±1% base-emitter. An independent active-high pulse drives it; the 47 kΩ discharges the base on release/reset, conditional on reset bias. At loaded VOH=2.64 V and assumed VBE=0.8 V, IB≈(2.64−0.8)/3333−0.8/46530=0.535 mA. Absolute drive demand ≤1.11 mA at 3.6 V. At 25 °C the vendor guarantees ≤0.25 V at IC=10 mA, IB=0.5 mA. Typical source gives only ~1.3 µA at 6 V. Neither this saturation point nor ICBO=15 nA at 25 °C / 5 µA at 150 °C guarantees the released current or asserted voltage at the required bias and 85 °C. No transistor or resistor fitted. [onsemi electrical table](https://www.onsemi.com/pdf/datasheet/bc847att1-d.pdf)
- **Upper evaluation:** VOMA617A-3X001T LED from independent 3.0–3.6 V wake supply through trial 249 Ω ±1%, cathode to reset-off sink on CELL0. Collector to U_BQ2.22, emitter to CELL16. Removing drive extinguishes the LED. At VF(5 mA)=1.5 V max at 25 °C and sink ≤0.2 V, (3−1.5−0.2)/251.49=5.17 mA available at the 5 mA test point. Even a shorted LED draws ≤14.61 mA. Verify loaded source, driver and resistor. CTR 100–200% at 5 mA, VCE=5 V; VCEsat ≤0.4 V at IF=5 mA, IC=1.25 mA, 25 °C. This gives 0.3 V to the target under those conditions. No guaranteed 85 °C VOL or dark-current limit for this application: 100 nA maximum is at 25 °C, VCE=50 V; Fig. 9 is typical. Repetitive peak working rating VIORM=707 Vpeak vs nominal worst half-stack 67.36 V. Verify actual repetitive/transient stress and PCB clearance/creepage; the 3750 Vrms one-minute withstand rating is not working voltage. No optocoupler, LED resistor or driver fitted. [Vishay 84433 pp. 3–5](https://www.vishay.com/docs/84433/voma617a.pdf)
- **Power and firmware:** both candidates draw control energy from a live host/wake supply independent of REG1. With HOST_3V3 off, a button or charger-powered one-shot must start a qualified host supply or generate both pulses independently. Neither source is established in main. Do not connect charger voltage to TS2. Raw TS2 ports stop on the root sheet. No spare GPIO assumed. Micro uses GPIO3 PWM and GPIO9, and the C target configures GPIO4/8 as legacy switch outputs and exposes setters. Evaluate reclaiming GPIO4 first, removing/guarding all prior owners; GPIO8 requires additional boot-strap analysis. Default must be base/LED off through reset and power loss. No firmware pin assigned.
- **Qualification for both:** command SHUTDOWN with valid BAT, LD below wake threshold, both REG1 zero. At min/max wake supply and −40/25/85 °C apply a trial 2 ms pulse; measure pin 22 relative to CELL0 or CELL16 with a suitably isolated low-leakage probe. Require <0.7 V continuously for ≥200 µs (project acceptance floor), release, confirm both monitors start and can re-enter SHUTDOWN. Repeat at minimum BAT, host off/reset and midpulse power loss. At 85 °C bias each assembled output at 6 V with guarded source meter, drive OFF. Require total leakage ≤0.10 µA including board/bias, >8× below illustrative threshold; verify released TS2 above 1.1 V and no false wake/soft-shutdown lockup on hot soak. Include instrument leakage. Obtain a hot vendor bound or qualified production screening before selection.
- After SHUTDOWN, wait for initialization/readiness, restore volatile configuration and address: GPIO9 high, verify lower at 0x10 before admitting upper at 0x08. Verify sensor/balance/config readback. DEEPSLEEP is a separate mode; use EXIT_DEEPSLEEP or a documented hardware exit, not ordinary serial reads.
